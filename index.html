Frequently Asked Questions

How can I start learning English on this website?
You can start learning English by exploring our vocabulary lessons, practicing new words, listening to their pronunciation, and completing interactive exercises.
Is this website free to use?
Yes, you can use the vocabulary lessons and practice features for free and start improving your English vocabulary right away.
Do I need to create an account?
No, you can start learning and practicing English vocabulary without creating an account.
How can I build my English vocabulary?
You can build your vocabulary by learning new words regularly, reviewing their meanings and pronunciations, and practicing them through different lessons and exercises.
Can I listen to the pronunciation of a word?
Yes, you can click the pronunciation button to listen to the correct pronunciation of a word and improve your speaking skills.
